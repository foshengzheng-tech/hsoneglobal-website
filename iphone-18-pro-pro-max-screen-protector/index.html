Screen Protector Solutions for iPhone 18 Pro & Pro Max

High-Aluminosilicate Tempered Glass

A strong core solution for everyday protection, combining high clarity, smooth touch, scratch resistance and impact protection. OEM options can include different thicknesses, edge treatments, oleophobic coatings, installation trays and custom packaging.

Anti-Reflective (AR) Screen Protector

Designed for premium users who want lower reflection and glare with a clearer viewing experience. AR screen protectors are well suited to high-end retail positioning and can help brands build a product tier above standard clear tempered glass.

A privacy-focused solution designed to reduce side viewing while maintaining a bright and clear front-view experience. It is suitable for business users, commuters and privacy-conscious consumers.

Precision Fit for a Premium User Experience

Fit is one of the most important factors in a successful screen protector launch. HSONE treats the sensor area, bezel clearance, case overlap and edge geometry as one precision tolerance system.

  • Precision cutting for the front sensor and display area.
  • Edge-to-edge or case-friendly coverage options for different channels.
  • Compatibility testing with representative thin, rugged and MagSafe cases.
  • High-transparency adhesive systems designed for stable bonding and clear viewing.
  • Alignment trays and positioning tools for fast, bubble-free installation.
  • Real-device validation before mass production whenever final devices are available.
