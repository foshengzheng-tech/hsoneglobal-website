Will iPhone 17 Pro Screen Protectors Fit iPhone 18 Pro?

Short Answer: Can iPhone 17 Pro Glass Fit iPhone 18 Pro? Possibly — but not every design should be treated as automatically compatible. Apple’s official specifications show that iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max keep the same overall body dimensions, display sizes and resolutions as iPhone 17 Pro and iPhone 17 Pro Max. That gives existing clear-glass tooling a strong starting point.

However, Apple has also redesigned the Dynamic Island and made it smaller on iPhone 18 Pro models. For black-border tempered glass, privacy glass, AR products, sensor-sensitive structures and installation trays, the top area must be re-verified before mass production. The safest OEM answer is: reuse what testing proves compatible — not what looks compatible on paper.

1. Official Size Comparison: iPhone 17 Pro vs iPhone 18 Pro

Apple’s official specifications show no change in the main body dimensions or display specifications between the two Pro generations:

  • iPhone 17 Pro & iPhone 18 Pro: 71.9 mm × 150.0 mm × 8.75 mm | 6.3" Display
  • iPhone 17 Pro Max & iPhone 18 Pro Max: 78.0 mm × 163.4 mm × 8.75 mm | 6.9" Display

This is commercially important: brands may be able to shorten development time by using iPhone 17 Pro / Pro Max protector tooling as an engineering reference. But Apple does not publish every detail of cover-glass curvature, active-area tolerance or accessory edge geometry in the basic device dimensions, so real-device fit approval is still necessary.

2. The Smaller Dynamic Island Is the Main Screen-Protector Change

Apple has officially confirmed that iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max use a smaller, redesigned Dynamic Island. For the screen protector industry, this is the part of the front panel that deserves the most attention:

  • Re-check the top cutout or black-border printing area.
  • Confirm that privacy optical layers do not reduce Face ID or front-camera performance.
  • Confirm the alignment tray still positions the protector correctly relative to the new top-area layout.

3. Which iPhone 17 Pro Screen Protectors May Be Reusable?

Clear Full-Adhesive Tempered Glass: This is the strongest candidate for reuse. Even then, confirm edge clearance and display coverage before approving the product as iPhone 18 Pro compatible.

Black-Border & Privacy Glass: Do not automatically reuse the old masking. A smaller Dynamic Island changes the sensor area relationship.
