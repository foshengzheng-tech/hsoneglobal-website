Stronger Privacy Without an Excessively Dark Front View

Privacy protection should not force users to choose between confidentiality and a usable display. HSONE develops premium privacy screen protectors for brands that want stronger side-view protection while maintaining practical front-view brightness, clarity and touch performance.

Our approved 20° Privacy configuration targets 68% visible light transmittance, creating a narrower privacy viewing zone while preserving a brighter front-facing experience. It is designed for premium privacy programs where optical performance matters as much as anti-peeping protection.

20° Privacy + 68% Light Transmittance

The key challenge in privacy screen protection is balance. A narrower viewing zone can improve side-view privacy, but an overly aggressive optical structure may also reduce front-view brightness or clarity. HSONE’s premium privacy platform is developed around measurable optical performance rather than privacy claims alone.

  • 20° privacy viewing specification on the approved HSONE configuration.
  • 68% visible light transmittance target for the approved configuration.
  • Clear front-facing viewing experience for everyday use.
  • Reduced side-angle visibility for greater visual privacy in public environments.
  • Smooth touch response and premium glass surface feel.
  • Compatible product development for major smartphone models and new-device launches.
