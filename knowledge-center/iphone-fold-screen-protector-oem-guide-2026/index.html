iPhone Fold Screen Protector: What Brands Need to Prepare Before Apple’s First Foldable iPhone

Apple’s first foldable iPhone is widely expected to become one of the most important premium accessory launches of 2026. Current reports continue to point to a September 2026 debut alongside the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max.

For screen protector brands, this is not simply another size extension. A foldable phone creates two protection jobs in one device: a conventional outer display and a flexible inner display that must survive repeated bending, crease stress, and adhesive movement.

1. iPhone Fold / iPhone Ultra: What Is Currently Expected?

  • Form factor: Book-style foldable is the dominant expectation.
  • Cover display: Approximately 5.4–5.5 inches.
  • Inner display: Approximately 7.7–7.8 inches.
  • Launch window: September 2026 (rumored).

2. Why Foldable Displays Need a Different Protection Strategy

Foldable displays are different because the protection stack must repeatedly bend around a hinge. The inner display is a mechanical system, not just a flat optical surface. Repeated bending concentrates strain near the crease, so thickness, modulus, and adhesive behavior matter.

  • Rigid tempered-glass is for the cover screen only.
  • Inner screens require flexible film systems (PET/TPU/Hydrogel).
  • Adhesive stability near the crease is the top failure point.

3. Two Screens, Two Product Strategies

Area Recommended Direction Critical Requirements
Outer Screen Tempered glass or reinforced glass Scratch resistance, impact protection, oleophobic coating.
Inner Screen Ultra-thin flexible film (PET/TPU/Hybrid) Fold durability, low crease stress, optical clarity.
Hinge Zone Reserved-gap or protective component No interference with travel, dust control.

4. Material Options: PET, TPU, Hydrogel and Hybrid

Hybrid / coated flexible structures are likely to be the most important OEM direction. Buyers want both foldability and a premium "iPhone-like" surface experience. HSONE is currently validating multiple flexible stacks to balance these requirements.

5. OEM Sampling Strategy for 2026

  1. Material Shortlist: Validate 2-3 inner-screen candidate films.
  2. Mechanical Prototypes: Test crease recovery and adhesion stability.
  3. Installation Design: Develop specialized alignment trays for the larger inner display.
  4. Dimension Lock: Finalize CAD files once production samples are verified.
