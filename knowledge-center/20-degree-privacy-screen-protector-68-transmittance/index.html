Industry-Exclusive 20° Privacy Screen Protector with 68% Light Transmittance: A New Standard for Premium Privacy Glass

HSONE combines a narrow 20° privacy viewing zone with 68% visible light transmittance for premium privacy screen protector programs.

Privacy screen protectors have become a core product category for mobile-accessory brands because smartphones now display banking information, business messages, personal photographs, and confidential documents in public environments. The commercial challenge is how to deliver stronger side-view privacy without making the screen look excessively dark from the front.

What Is a 20° Privacy Screen Protector?

A 20° privacy screen protector is an anti-spy screen protector designed to keep the display visible within a narrow front-facing zone and substantially darker when viewed from the side. The effect is created by a precision micro-louver optical structure that guides light toward the intended user while restricting off-axis observers.

Under a consistent method, a 20-degree privacy specification represents a narrower usable viewing zone than a 28-degree or 30-degree product. This makes it attractive for customers who frequently use their phones in crowded places.

Why 68% Light Transmittance Matters

Privacy performance and light transmittance usually work against each other. HSONE's 20° privacy platform targets 68% light transmittance. The commercial importance of this number is the balance it represents: a narrow privacy zone combined with brighter front-facing visibility.

  • Brighter front-facing readability: Users can read documents and pay more comfortably.
  • Better premium positioning: Supports a stronger product story than generic glass.
  • More balanced daily usability: Practical for everyday communication and work.
